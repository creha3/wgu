Plan Your Trip

Get to Taniti by air or cruise ship, then choose buses, taxis, or bicycles to explore once you arrive.

Getting to Taniti

Most visitors arrive by air into Taniti’s airport. A small cruise ship docks once per week in Yellow Leaf Bay.

Ground Transportation

Public buses run in Taniti City from 5 a.m. to 11 p.m. Taxis, rental cars, and bike rentals with helmets are available.

Safety & Medical Care

Violent crime is rare, but petty theft can occur in busy areas. There is one hospital and several clinics on the island.

Money & Power

Taniti uses the U.S. dollar and many businesses accept euros and yen. Power outlets are 120 volts.

Holidays & Closures

Taniti has many national holidays; some restaurants and attractions may be closed. Check dates before booking activities.

Language Tips

Many younger residents speak English in Taniti City, but English is less common in rural areas.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Taniti kid-friendly?

Yes. Many beaches are calm and shallow, and several hotels and resorts offer kid-friendly amenities, kids’ clubs, and family rooms.

What is there to do for single adults?

Merriton Landing has pubs, a microbrewery, a dance club, late movies, bowling, and art galleries—great for solo travelers and groups of adults.

Do I need a car?

Not necessarily. Taniti City is walkable with public buses, taxis, and bike rentals. A car can be useful if you want to explore the rest of the island.

What about payment methods?

The U.S. dollar is the main currency, but some businesses accept euros and yen. Most major credit cards are accepted.
